Chicken Noodle Soup

Ingredients:

  • Carrots
  • Celery
  • 1 Onion
  • Rotisserie Chicken
  • Soup Mix Bag

Instructions:

Simmer chopped vegetables with soup mix and shredded rotisserie chicken until tender. Serve hot.
