Protein Balls

Ingredients:

  • 1 Cup Protein Oats
  • ½ Cup Peanut Butter
  • ½ Cup Flax Seed
  • ½ Cup Chocolate Chips
  • ⅓ Cup Honey
  • 1 Teaspoon Vanilla Extract

Instructions:

Mix all ingredients in a bowl until well combined. Chill for 1–2 hours. Roll into balls and refrigerate.
