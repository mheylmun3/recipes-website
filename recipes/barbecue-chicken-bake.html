Barbecue Chicken Bake

Ingredients:

  • Butter
  • Chicken Breasts
  • Barbecue Sauce
  • Bacon
  • Cheese
  • Cooking Spray

Instructions:

Sear seasoned chicken in butter. Transfer to greased baking dish. Top with BBQ sauce and cheese. Bake at 350°F for 30 minutes. Top with cooked bacon and serve.
