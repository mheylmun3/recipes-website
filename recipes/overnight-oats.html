Overnight Oats

Ingredients:

  • Oats
  • Peanut Butter
  • Chia Seeds
  • Protein Milk
  • Apples or Other Fruit
  • Flax Seed

Instructions:

Combine oats, peanut butter, chia seeds, protein milk, and flax seed. Add chopped fruit. Refrigerate overnight before serving.
