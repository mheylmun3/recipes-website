Beef and Broccoli

Ingredients:

  • Coleslaw Mix
  • Broccoli
  • Beef
  • Omson Lemongrass Marinade
  • Rice Ramen

Instructions:

Brown beef with ginger, garlic, salt, and pepper. Add coleslaw mix and sauce. Cook rice ramen separately. Combine beef mix with noodles and broccoli. Serve hot.
