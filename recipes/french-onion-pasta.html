French Onion Pasta

Ingredients:

  • 1-2 Onions
  • 2 Tablespoons Butter
  • Gruyere Cheese
  • White Wine
  • Uncooked Pasta
  • Beef Stock
  • Worcestershire Sauce
  • Half and Half
  • Thyme

Instructions:

Caramelize onions in butter, then deglaze with white wine. Add pasta, beef stock, Worcestershire, and thyme. Simmer until pasta is cooked. Stir in gruyere and half and half.
