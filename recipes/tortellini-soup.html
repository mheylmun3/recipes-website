Tortellini Soup

Ingredients:

  • Carrots
  • Celery
  • 1 Onion
  • Chicken Broth
  • Tortellini
  • Cream
  • Flour or Ranch Seasoning Mix
  • Italian Sausage

Instructions:

Saute carrots, celery, and onion. Add Italian sausage and cook through. Stir in chicken broth, flour or ranch mix, and tortellini. Simmer until pasta is tender. Add cream and serve.
