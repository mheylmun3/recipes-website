Carnitas

Ingredients:

  • Pork Butt or Shoulder
  • Chicken Broth
  • Orange Juice
  • Lime Juice
  • Green Chilis
  • Onion
  • Tortillas
  • Cayenne Pepper
  • Chili Powder
  • Cumin
  • Dried Cilantro

Instructions:

Season and sear pork. Add to crockpot with broth, juices, chilis, onion, and seasonings. Cook on low for 7-8 hours. Shred and serve in tortillas.
