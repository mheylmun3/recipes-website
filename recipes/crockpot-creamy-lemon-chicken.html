Crockpot Creamy Lemon Chicken

Ingredients:

  • Chicken Breasts
  • Butter
  • Lemon Juice and Zest
  • Garlic
  • Chicken Stock
  • Half and Half
  • Cornstarch
  • Chicken Base

Instructions:

Sear chicken in butter, then add to crockpot with lemon juice, zest, garlic, and sliced butter. Deglaze pan with stock and add to crockpot. Cook on low for 4 hours. Mix half and half, cornstarch, and chicken base. Add and cook 1 hour more.
