Parmesan-Crusted Oven Roasted Potatoes

Ingredients:

  • ¼ Cup Butter
  • ¼ Cup Olive Oil
  • 1 Tablespoon Garlic
  • 2 Tablespoons Parsley
  • 3 Tablespoons Parmesan
  • Red Pepper Flakes
  • Salt and Pepper
  • 2 Pounds Baby Gold Potatoes

Instructions:

Preheat oven to 400°F. Melt butter and mix with olive oil, garlic, parsley, parmesan, red pepper flakes, salt, and pepper. Slice potatoes in half and toss in the mixture. Dip the flat side of each potato in extra parmesan and lay crust side down on a baking sheet. Roast for 30–40 minutes until golden.
