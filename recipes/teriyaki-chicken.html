Teriyaki Chicken

Ingredients:

  • Chicken Breasts
  • Garlic
  • Ginger
  • Honey
  • Brown Sugar
  • Soy Sauce
  • Sesame Oil
  • Rice Vinegar
  • Cornstarch

Instructions:

Place chicken in crockpot. Mix garlic, ginger, honey, sugar, soy sauce, oil, and vinegar. Pour over chicken. Cook low 6-7 hours or high 3-4. Thicken sauce with cornstarch slurry if needed.
