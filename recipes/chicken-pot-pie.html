Chicken Pot Pie

Ingredients:

  • Chicken Breast
  • Diced Onion
  • Cream of Chicken Soup
  • Frozen Vegetables
  • Biscuits
  • Half and Half

Instructions:

Add chicken, onion, cream of chicken soup, and vegetables to crockpot. Cook on low 4-6 hours. Stir in half and half. Serve with biscuits.
