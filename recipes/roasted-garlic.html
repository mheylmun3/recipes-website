Roasted Garlic

Ingredients:

  • Whole Garlic Heads
  • Olive Oil
  • Salt and Pepper
  • Aluminum Foil

Instructions:

Preheat oven to 400°F. Trim tops of garlic heads to expose cloves. Drizzle with olive oil and season with salt and pepper. Wrap in foil and roast for 30–40 minutes until soft and golden.
