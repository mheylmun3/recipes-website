Chicken Enchilada Soup

Ingredients:

  • Rotisserie Chicken
  • Corn
  • Tomatoes
  • Black Beans
  • Soup Mix
  • Cheese

Instructions:

Combine shredded rotisserie chicken, corn, tomatoes, black beans, and soup mix in a pot. Simmer until heated through. Top with cheese before serving.
