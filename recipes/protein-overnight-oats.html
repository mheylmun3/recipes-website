Protein Overnight Oats

Ingredients:

  • ½ Cup Oats
  • ½ Cup Milk
  • 1 Scoop Protein Powder
  • ½ Cup Greek Yogurt
  • Fruit

Instructions:

Combine all ingredients and refrigerate overnight. Stir and top with fruit before eating.
