Ricotta Meatballs

Ingredients:

  • Olive Oil
  • Tomato Sauce
  • Ricotta Cheese
  • Garlic
  • Fresh Parsley
  • Dried Basil, Oregano, Parsley
  • Onion
  • Egg
  • Half and Half
  • Italian Breadcrumbs
  • Fresh Parmesan Cheese
  • Ground Chuck
  • Ground Italian Sausage

Instructions:

Mix ricotta, garlic, and seasonings. Sauté onion and garlic in olive oil. In a bowl, combine egg, half and half, breadcrumbs, cheese, ricotta mix, sautéed onion. Add seasoned meat and form into meatballs. Refrigerate 15 min. Brown in oil, then add to sauce and cook covered 30 min. Add remaining ricotta on top. Serve with pasta.
