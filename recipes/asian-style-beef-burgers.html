Asian-Style Beef Burgers

Ingredients:

  • Ground Beef
  • Potato Buns
  • Garlic
  • Scallion
  • Dried Shiitake Mushrooms
  • Carrots
  • Baby Bok Choy
  • Broccoli
  • Hoisin Sauce
  • Sesame Oil
  • Apple Cider Vinegar
  • Kombu

Instructions:

Rehydrate mushrooms. Sauté carrots and broccoli in sesame oil. Add water and vinegar, cook until soft. Make patties from beef, scallions, mushrooms, garlic, hoisin. Sear patties. Toast buns. Assemble with bok choy and cooked veggies. Garnish with scallion tops.
