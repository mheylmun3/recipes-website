Gigi Hadid Pasta

Ingredients:

  • Shallots
  • Tomato Paste
  • Pasta
  • Heavy Cream
  • Olive Oil
  • Butter
  • Parmesan
  • Red Pepper Flakes

Instructions:

Cook pasta and reserve 1 cup pasta water. In a pan, sauté shallots and garlic in olive oil. Add tomato paste and cook 2-3 minutes. Add heavy cream and red pepper flakes, then stir in pasta, reserved water, butter, and parmesan. Season and serve.
