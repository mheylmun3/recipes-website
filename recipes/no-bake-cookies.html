No Bake Cookies

Ingredients:

  • 1 Cup Sugar
  • ¼ Cup Milk
  • 4 Tablespoons Butter
  • ⅛ Cup Cocoa Powder
  • ½ Tablespoon Vanilla Extract
  • 1½ Cups Oats
  • ½ Cup Peanut Butter
  • Parchment Paper

Instructions:

In a saucepan, melt sugar, milk, butter, and cocoa powder. Bring to a boil for 1 minute. Remove from heat and stir in vanilla, oats, and peanut butter. Scoop onto parchment-lined baking sheet and refrigerate until firm.
