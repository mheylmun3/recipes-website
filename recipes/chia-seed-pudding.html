Chia Seed Pudding

Ingredients:

  • 2 Tablespoons Chia Seeds
  • ¼ Cup Water or Milk
  • ¾ Cup Greek Yogurt
  • 1 Scoop Protein Powder
  • 1 Tablespoon of Peanut Butter (optional)
  • Fruit

Instructions:

Mix all ingredients and let sit overnight in the fridge. Top with fruit before serving.
