Protein Muffins

Ingredients:

  • 1 Egg
  • ¼ Cup Apple Sauce
  • ½ Cup Brown Sugar
  • ¼ Stick Butter
  • ¾ Cup Protein Milk
  • ½ Tablespoon Cinnamon
  • Salt
  • 1.5 Cups Protein Rolled Oats
  • 1 Teaspoon Baking Powder
  • Chocolate Chips
  • Flax Seed or Hemp Hearts
  • Muffin Liners

Instructions:

Preheat oven to 350°F. Line muffin pan with liners. In a bowl, mix brown sugar, egg, and melted butter. Add applesauce, protein milk, cinnamon, and salt. Stir in oats and baking powder. Pour into muffin liners using a ¼ cup measure. Top with chocolate chips, flax seed, or hemp hearts. Bake for 30 minutes.
