Chocolate Chip Cookies

Ingredients:

  • ½ Cup Sugar
  • ½ Cup Butter
  • 1 Cup Brown Sugar
  • 1 Egg
  • 1 Teaspoon Vanilla Extract
  • 1 Teaspoon Salt
  • 1¼ Cups Flour
  • ½ Teaspoon Baking Soda
  • 8 Ounces Chocolate Chips

Instructions:

Cream sugars and butter. Add egg and vanilla. In another bowl, combine salt, flour, and baking soda. Mix dry into wet. Fold in chocolate chips. Chill dough 2 hours. Bake at 250°F for 8–13 minutes.
