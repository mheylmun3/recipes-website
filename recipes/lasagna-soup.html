Lasagna Soup

Ingredients:

  • Onion
  • Garlic
  • 1 Pound Italian Sausage
  • Tomato Paste
  • 2 Cups Marinara
  • 3 Cups Chicken Broth
  • ½ Cup Heavy Cream
  • ½ Box Lasagna Noodles
  • Parmesan, Mozzarella, Ricotta
  • Parsley, Spinach, and Basil

Instructions:

Saute onion and garlic, then brown Italian sausage. Add tomato paste, marinara, broth, and heavy cream. Add noodles and cook until tender. Serve topped with mixed cheeses and herbs.
