Stir Fry

Ingredients:

  • Soy Sauce
  • Vegetable Stock
  • Sesame Oil
  • Rice Vinegar
  • Garlic
  • Grated Ginger
  • Sugar
  • Cornstarch
  • Red Pepper Flakes
  • Bell Peppers
  • Onion
  • Snap Peas
  • Stir Fry Meat

Instructions:

Make sauce by mixing soy sauce, stock, oil, vinegar, garlic, ginger, sugar, cornstarch, and red pepper. Stir fry meat and set aside. Sauté onion, snap peas, and peppers. Add meat back and pour sauce over. Simmer until thickened. Serve with rice or noodles.
