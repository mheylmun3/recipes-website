Parmesan-Garlic Butter Pizza Rolls

Ingredients:

  • Pizza Rolls
  • Garlic Butter
  • Parmesan Cheese
  • Marinara Sauce

Instructions:

Fry pizza rolls until crisp. Toss in garlic butter and top with parmesan. Serve with marinara sauce for dipping.
