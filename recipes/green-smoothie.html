Green Smoothie

Ingredients:

  • Cucumber
  • Spinach
  • Celery
  • Avocado
  • Hemp Seeds or Chia Seeds
  • Protein Powder
  • Water
  • Pineapple
  • ½ Apple
  • ½ Banana
  • Ginger

Instructions:

Blend all ingredients together until smooth. Adjust thickness by adding more water if needed. Serve chilled.
