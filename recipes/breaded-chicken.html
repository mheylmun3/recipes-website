Breaded Chicken

Ingredients:

  • Chicken Tenderloins
  • Flour
  • Eggs
  • Panko Breadcrumbs
  • Italian Breadcrumbs
  • Grated Parmesan
  • Olive Oil

Instructions:

Dredge chicken in flour, then egg, then breadcrumb and cheese mix. Shallow fry in olive oil for 3-4 minutes per side until golden and cooked.
