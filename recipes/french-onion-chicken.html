French Onion Chicken

Ingredients:

  • 1 Onion
  • 1 Stick Butter
  • Chicken Breasts or Tenderloins
  • Onion Soup Mix
  • French Onion Soup
  • Potatoes
  • Heavy Cream
  • Cheese

Instructions:

Sear chicken in butter, then set aside. Saute onions, then add soup mix and canned soup. Return chicken and simmer. Top with cheese and serve with potatoes.
