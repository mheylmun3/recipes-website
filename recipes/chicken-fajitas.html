Chicken Fajitas

Ingredients:

  • Peppers/Onions
  • Chicken
  • Fajita Seasoning
  • Tortillas
  • Cheese
  • Rice

Instructions:

Add sliced peppers, onions, and chicken to crockpot. Sprinkle fajita seasoning over and cook on low for 6-8 hours or high for 3-4 hours. Serve in tortillas with cheese and rice.
