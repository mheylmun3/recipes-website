Lemon Chicken Orzo Soup

Ingredients:

  • Carrots
  • Celery
  • Onion
  • Butter
  • Garlic
  • Orzo
  • Flour
  • Chicken Broth
  • Rotisserie Chicken
  • Lemon

Instructions:

Saute carrots, celery, and onion in butter. Add garlic and flour, then stir in broth. Bring to a boil, add orzo, and cook until tender. Add shredded chicken and lemon juice/zest before serving.
