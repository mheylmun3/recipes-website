Chicken and Dumplings

Ingredients:

  • Chicken Breast
  • Onion
  • Celery
  • Carrots
  • Cream of Chicken Soup
  • Cream of Celery or Onion Soup
  • Chicken Broth
  • Biscuits
  • Ranch Seasoning

Instructions:

Add chicken, vegetables, soups, broth, and seasoning to crockpot. Cook on high for 4 hours. Shred chicken and add biscuits on top before serving.
