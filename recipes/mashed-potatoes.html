Mashed Potatoes

Ingredients:

  • 3–4 Potatoes
  • Butter
  • Cream
  • Garlic Powder
  • Salt and Pepper

Instructions:

Peel and cube potatoes. Boil in salted water until fork-tender. Drain and return to pot. Mash with butter, cream, garlic powder, salt, and pepper to taste.
