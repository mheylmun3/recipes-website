Soupy Chicken

Ingredients:

  • Chicken Breasts
  • Olive Oil
  • Butter
  • Garlic
  • 2 Onions
  • Cream of Onion or Chicken
  • Milk
  • Mashed Potatoes Ingredients
  • Green Beans

Instructions:

Sear seasoned chicken and onions in butter and oil. Add soup and milk. Simmer until chicken is cooked through. Serve with mashed potatoes and green beans.
