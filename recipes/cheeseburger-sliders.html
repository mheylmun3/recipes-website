Cheeseburger Sliders with Mac n Cheese

Ingredients:

  • Ground Beef
  • Hamburger Buns
  • Cheese
  • Onion

Instructions:

Form ground beef into small patties and cook with sliced onion. Toast buns and assemble with cheese. Serve with mac n cheese if desired.
