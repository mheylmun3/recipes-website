Crack Chicken Noodle Soup

Ingredients:

  • 1 Onion
  • Carrot
  • Celery
  • Chicken Stock
  • Cream of Chicken or Onion Soup
  • Spaghetti
  • Rotisserie Chicken
  • Cheddar Cheese
  • Ranch Seasoning Mix

Instructions:

Combine all ingredients except cheese in a large pot and bring to a boil. Simmer until vegetables and pasta are tender. Stir in cheddar cheese and serve.
