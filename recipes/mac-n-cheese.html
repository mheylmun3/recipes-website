Mac n Cheese

Ingredients:

  • 1 Pound Cavatappi Noodles
  • 1 Pound Mozzarella Cheese
  • 1 Pound Colby Jack Cheese
  • ½ Pound Sharp Cheddar Cheese
  • Salt
  • Pepper
  • Garlic Powder
  • Smoked Paprika
  • 3 Tablespoons Butter
  • 3 Tablespoons Flour
  • 1 Can Evaporated Milk
  • 2 Cups Heavy Cream

Instructions:

Cook and drain noodles. Shred and divide cheeses. Melt butter and add flour to make a roux. Stir in evaporated milk and heavy cream until thickened. Season and add half of cheese. Combine with noodles and layer remaining cheese. Bake at 350°F for 25–30 minutes.
