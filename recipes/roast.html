Roast

Ingredients:

  • Chuck Roast
  • Potatoes
  • Onion
  • Carrots
  • Beef Stock
  • Pot Roast Seasoning or Onion Mix

Instructions:

Place roast in crockpot and surround with chopped potatoes, onions, and carrots. Add seasoning and beef stock. Cook on low for 8-10 hours or high for 4-5 hours.
