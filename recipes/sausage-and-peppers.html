Sausage and Peppers

Ingredients:

  • Sausage
  • 1 Onion
  • Peppers
  • Olive Oil
  • Garlic

Instructions:

Sear sausage, then set aside. Saute onion and garlic, then add peppers and cook until tender. Return sausage and heat through. Serve with pasta if desired.
