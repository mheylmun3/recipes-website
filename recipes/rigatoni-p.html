Rigatoni P

Ingredients:

  • Sliced Mushrooms
  • Diced Onion
  • Garlic
  • Balsamic Vinegar
  • Cornstarch
  • Chicken Broth
  • Rigatoni Pasta
  • Olive Oil
  • Butter
  • Chicken Breast
  • White Wine
  • Marsala Wine
  • Heavy Cream
  • Fresh Parmesan Cheese
  • Fresh Parsley

Instructions:

Preheat oven to 450°F. Roast mushrooms, onion, and garlic with balsamic vinegar. Sear chicken in butter and oil. Deglaze with white and marsala wine. Add chicken broth and cornstarch slurry, cook until thick. Mix in cream, cheese, parsley, and roasted vegetables. Combine with cooked pasta and bake until golden.
