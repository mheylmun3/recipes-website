Crockpot Chicken Alfredo

Ingredients:

  • Chicken Breast
  • 2 Cups Heavy Cream
  • 1.5 Cups Chicken Broth
  • 4 Ounces Cream Cheese
  • 4 Tablespoons Butter
  • 1 Pound Pasta
  • ½ Cup Parmesan

Instructions:

Add chicken, cream, broth, cream cheese, and butter to crockpot. Cook on high for 2 hours. Shred chicken and add pasta and parmesan. Cook 30 minutes more.
