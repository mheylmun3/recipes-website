Loaded Potato Soup

Ingredients:

  • Celery
  • ½ Onion
  • Garlic
  • 4 Potatoes
  • Bacon
  • Flour
  • Vegetable Stock
  • Vegetable Bullion
  • Cheddar Cheese
  • Heavy Cream

Instructions:

Fry bacon, then saute celery, onion, and garlic in bacon fat. Add flour, vegetable stock, and bullion. Add cubed potatoes and simmer until tender. Stir in heavy cream and top with cheese and crumbled bacon.
